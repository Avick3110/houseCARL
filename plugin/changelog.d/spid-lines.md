- **`housecarl_layer` now reads SPID's `_DISTR.ini` files as SPID 7.3 does: `formids=` / `plugins=` list the SPID lines naming those records (target, filter or value), and `filter=` expands a file's lines with what SPID does with each.** With `distributor` omitted (or `[]`) a call now reads SPID too: SkyPatcher's answer comes first, as long as it was, and SPID's part takes what is left of `max_chars`, saying where it is cut. With `formids=`, `plugins=`, or a `filter=` that matches a SPID file, such a call resolves every SPID line, so it is slower (on a test order 0.7 s became about 6 s); `distributor=["skypatcher"]` gives the answer and speed it gave before. Check: `housecarl_layer distributor=["spid"] filter="<mod>"`.
