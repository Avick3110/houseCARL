- **`housecarl_layer` now reads SPID's `_DISTR.ini` files as SPID 7.3 does: `formids=` / `plugins=` list the SPID lines naming those records (target, filter or value), and `filter=` expands a file's lines with what SPID does with each.** With `distributor` omitted (or `[]`) a call now reads SPID too: SkyPatcher's answer comes first, as long as it was, and SPID's part takes what is left of `max_chars`, saying where it is cut; at a `max_chars` SkyPatcher's answer fills, the answer runs past it by SPID's header, its cut and a notice, and both notices name `distributor=["spid"]`, which reads SPID alone. With `formids=`, `plugins=`, or a `filter=` that matches a SPID file, such a call resolves every SPID line: on a test order 0.7 s became about 6 s, and on ARR `filter="npc"` went from 2.1 to about 9.5 s, while `plugins=` calls went from about 23 to about 10 s, as SkyPatcher's own EditorID lookup now takes one pass. `distributor=["skypatcher"]` gives the answer it gave before, on ARR faster. Check: `housecarl_layer distributor=["spid"] filter="<mod>"`.
